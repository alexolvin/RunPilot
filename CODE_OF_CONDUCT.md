# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We aim to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of experience level, gender, gender identity, disability,
ethnicity, age, or background.

## Our standards

Behavior that contributes to a positive environment includes:

- Being kind and constructive
- Respecting different viewpoints and approaches
- Giving and gracefully accepting feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information (e.g. home address, IP addresses,
  personal or infrastructure details) without explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and
may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, or issues that are not
in line with this Code of Conduct.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the project maintainer. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies both within project spaces and in public spaces
when an individual is representing the project.
